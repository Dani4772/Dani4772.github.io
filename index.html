About me

I'm a mobile app engineer with 4+ years of experience building, publishing, and maintaining cross-platform and native Android apps. That includes 3+ years of Flutter and Dart in production and 1+ year of hands-on native Android work in Kotlin and Java.

My apps span religious and lifestyle tools, e-commerce, social, media, education, weather, and community safety. I turn Figma designs into polished UI, wire up the APIs and Firebase behind it, and handle the release process myself.
